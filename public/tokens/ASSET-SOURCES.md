# Token logo sources

All supported token marks are bundled locally and selected by the shared asset catalog in `src/catalog.ts`.

- `aave.svg`, `ada.svg`, `avax.svg`, `bnb.svg`, `btc.svg`, `doge.svg`, `dot.svg`, `eth.svg`, `link.svg`, `ltc.svg`, `pepe.svg`, `shib.svg`, `sol.svg`, `sui.svg`, `trx.svg`, `usdc.svg`, `usdt.svg`, and `xrp.svg` are the branded SVG variants from `@web3icons/core` version 4.0.55, repository [0xa3k5/web3icons](https://github.com/0xa3k5/web3icons), distributed under the MIT license. The required notice is included in [`WEB3ICONS-LICENCE.txt`](./WEB3ICONS-LICENCE.txt).
- `hype.png` and `ton.png` were downloaded from CoinPaprika's public logo resources on 2026-11-01: [Hyperliquid](https://static.coinpaprika.com/coin/hype-hyperliquid/logo.png) and [Toncoin](https://static.coinpaprika.com/coin/ton-tontoken/logo.png).

All cryptocurrency names and logos are identifiers; their project and trademark rights remain with their respective owners. Logos load from the local app bundle; the runtime market quote service does not fetch token images.
